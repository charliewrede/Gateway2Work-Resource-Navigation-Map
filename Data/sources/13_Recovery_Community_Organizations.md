## 2. What was mapped (address from the organization's own site or several agreeing listings)
- **Advocates for Recovery Colorado:** headquarters, 3440 W 71st Pl, Westminster, and Aurora hub, 1445 Dayton St (advocatesforrecovery.org). Described by CARS as Colorado's first peer-run RCO and BHA-licensed.
- **Serenity Recovery Connection:** 985 W Fillmore St, Colorado Springs (ColoradoGives, Detox.com, Gazette agree). CAPRSS accredited.
- **Free Recovery Community (Free Café):** 2122 S Lafayette St, Denver (freerecoverycommunity.org).

## 1. Is there a statewide dataset?
No. Organizations were identified from sources that name Colorado **employment social enterprises / transitional jobs** programs:
- Colorado Attorney General, *Reentry Report* (2021, coag.gov), which names Bayaud Enterprises and Mile High WorkShop.
- Rose Community Foundation donor learning session handout (Feb 2024), which names Women's Bean Project and Bayaud.
- Goodwill of Colorado (administers the state's **ReHire Colorado** transitional jobs program, C.R.S. 26-2-1101 et seq.).
- Colorado Politics (May 2025) on Center for Employment Opportunities.

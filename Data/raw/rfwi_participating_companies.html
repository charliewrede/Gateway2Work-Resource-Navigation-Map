    Absolute Caulking & Waterproofing

    “Partnering with the Colorado Recovery Friendly Workplace Initiative™ provides us with resources and support, giving us confidence and structure for handling difficult situations. This partnership saves us time and resources, which is invaluable for our small, family-owned business.”

    – Sarah Deering

    Vice President

    “Going to a recovery program was life-changing for me. Absolute Caulking and Waterproofing checked in on me every week, holding me accountable and encouraging me to keep going. Getting sober made me realize how much I was missing out on, like being present with my family.”

    – Charlie

    Employee

    Odie B's

    “Partnering with the Colorado RFW furthers our mission to ensure a healthy workplace culture. We believe that if we take care of the folks inside our four walls, they can take care of our guests, which can extend to our community. It’s been wonderful to stand beside and support our employees in recovery.” ​

    – Cara & Cliff Blauvelt

    Owners

    “Odie B’s provides resources and a supportive workplace that helps me focus on my recovery while excelling at my job.

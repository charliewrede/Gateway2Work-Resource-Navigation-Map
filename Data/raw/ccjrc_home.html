OTHER CURRENT CAMPAIGNS

Community Reinvestment

We advocate for shifting public dollars away from incarceration and toward the housing, health care, and community-led solutions that prevent harm and strengthen communities.

LEARN MORE →

Get Real Denver

In Denver, we’re changing the narrative on public safety, pushing back against punitive policies, and winning meaningful investment in community-led solutions.

LEARN MORE →

No More Prisons Colorado

Crime is declining — so why is Colorado on track to open another prison? We’re organizing to stop expansion and advance policies that safely reduce incarceration.

LEARN MORE →

Voting with Conviction

Democracy includes everyone. CCJRC works to expand, protect, and implement the voting rights of justice-involved Coloradans.

LEARN MORE →

2026 LEGISLATIVE SESSION

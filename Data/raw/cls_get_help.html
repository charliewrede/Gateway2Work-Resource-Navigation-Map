How Do I Ask for Help?



Call or Visit Us

You can find us in a community near you to call or visit. If you call or visit us, we can help you in your native language.



Visit an Eviction Clinic

Click here or scroll down this page to find out if we offer in-person help at your courthouse on the day of your eviction summons.



Apply Online

Click here to fill out a pre-application online

Once we hear from you, a member of our team will need to talk to you about your eligibility for our services and if any resources are available. All applicants must complete an intake process.

In-Person Eviction Clinics

We provide in-person legal help for eviction cases at certain courthouses across the state. Our attorneys and paralegals there can meet with tenants to talk about their pending case. If you qualify and we have availability, we may be able to represent you or give you legal advice.

Important: Help is only available at the courthouse listed on your summons and on the same day as your court date. Be sure to bring your summons and any documents related to your case, and try to arrive early.

Adams County

8-11 a.m. Wednesday and Friday
Justice Center
6th floor, next to Division 1, Room 626
1100 Judicial Center Drive
Brighton

Arapahoe County

9:30 a.m.-1:30 p.m. Tuesday and Thursday
1790 West Littleton Blvd., Littleton
2nd floor outside courtroom A2

Broomfield County

8-11:30 a.m. Thursday
Broomfield County Courthouse, 2nd floor
17 Descombes Drive, Broomfield

Applicants should check in downstairs with the clerks, who can direct them to the clinic.

Denver County

People with public housing, project-based section 8, or housing choice vouchers only
8:30-11 a.m. Monday, Wednesday, and Friday
City and County Building, Room 179
1437 Bannock Street
Denver

Other organizations are available at the courthouse every weekday morning to help with evictions.

El Paso County

8:30-11:30 a.m. Tuesday and Thursday
270 South Tejon St, Room W-165
Colorado Springs

Larimer County

Fort Collins

Pueblo County Courthouse
501 N Elizabeth St, Pueblo
